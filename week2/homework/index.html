Double Chocolate Zucchini Bread

This bread is fudgy and soft, with chocolate chips mixed in and sprinkled on top. The zucchini keeps it moist without changing the flavor, so nobody can tell it's there.

Category

Quick Bread

Method

Oven

Cuisine

American

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups shredded zucchini
  • 1/4 cup Greek yogurt or sour cream
  • 1/3 cup vegetable oil
  • 1/2 cup packed light brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 2 large eggs, at room temperature
  • 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 cup Dutch-processed cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup semisweet chocolate chips, divided

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Grate the zucchini, wrap it in a paper towel, and press out some of the moisture. Measure 1 1/2 packed cups.
  3. In a large bowl, whisk the yogurt, oil, and both sugars. Add the vanilla, then the eggs one at a time. Stir in the zucchini.
  4. In a separate bowl, mix the flour, cocoa, baking soda, baking powder, and salt. Stir into the wet ingredients until no flour lumps remain. Fold in 2/3 cup of the chocolate chips.
  5. Pour the batter into a loaf pan and sprinkle the remaining chips on top. Bake for 45 minutes, or until a knife in the center comes out clean.
